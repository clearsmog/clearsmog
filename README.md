I was a trading assistant on ENN's LNG derivatives desk and then did an MSc at the
University of Edinburgh. My open-source work is mostly risk and pricing tooling of the
kind a desk runs every day. Each project is built end to end, runs on synthetic or public
data, and is tested.

## Risk and pricing

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [**derivatives-risk-engine**](https://github.com/clearsmog/derivatives-risk-engine) | Daily market-risk batch for a multi-asset book: pricing, Greeks, full-revaluation VaR/ES, P&L explain, FRTB PLA and SA capital, loaded into Atoti cubes |
| [**otc-valuation-desk**](https://github.com/clearsmog/otc-valuation-desk) | Independent price verification for GBP/USD rates and FX: OIS curves, own pricers checked against QuantLib, dealer-mark attribution |
| [**creditlab**](https://github.com/clearsmog/creditlab) | Corporate credit risk from SEC EDGAR filings: PD scorecard, ML and Merton models, portfolio loss, IFRS 9 ECL, Streamlit dashboard |

## Energy markets

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [**energy-forecasting-to-dispatch**](https://github.com/clearsmog/energy-forecasting-to-dispatch) | Day-ahead price and probabilistic load forecasting fed into battery bidding, to measure what a better forecast is actually worth |
| [**power-fundamentals**](https://github.com/clearsmog/power-fundamentals) | Solar and wind capture rates, negative prices, merit order and clean spark spreads for DE, ES and FR (2023–2026) |
